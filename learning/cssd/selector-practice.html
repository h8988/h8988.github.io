Middle-earth: A Guide

The World of Tolkien

Middle-earth is the setting of much of Tolkien's work. It is a vast land filled with many races, languages, and cultures.

The Lord of the Rings is one of the best-selling books ever written, with over 150 million copies sold worldwide.

The story follows the hobbit Frodo Baggins as he journeys to destroy the One Ring and defeat the Dark Lord Sauron.

Characters

Frodo Baggins

The Ring Bearer. A young hobbit from the Shire who is tasked with carrying the One Ring to Mount Doom.

Gandalf

A powerful wizard and member of the Fellowship. He guides and protects the hobbits on their journey.

Aragorn

The heir to the throne of Gondor. A skilled ranger known as Strider who fights to restore his kingdom.

Did you know? Aragorn is 87 years old during the events of The Lord of the Rings!
